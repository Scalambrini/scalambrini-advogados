The Firm

Advisory and litigation in two complementary practice groups.

Scalambrini e Saraiva Advogados advises and represents companies of different sizes and sectors, trade associations and individuals through two complementary practice groups: Tax and Customs, and Civil and Corporate.

The practice combines prevention and defense. In advisory work, transactions and risks are reviewed before decisions are made. In litigation, the firm handles administrative and judicial proceedings at every level, with strategy shaped by each matter’s impact on the client.

Service is direct and personalized, with close follow-up on every matter. Based on Avenida Engenheiro Luiz Carlos Berrini in São Paulo, the firm also works in Brasília.

Practice Areas

Practice areas

Tax Law

Advisory and litigation on federal, state and municipal taxes, including tax assessments, tax enforcement actions, annulment actions and writs of mandamus, at the administrative and judicial levels. Also covers ICMS tax incentives, social security and third-party contributions on payroll, including RAT and FAP, tax settlements and individual income tax.

Civil and Corporate Law

Strategic civil and commercial litigation, from first instance to the superior courts, and advice on corporate law, commercial contracts, holding structures and estate planning. Includes debt recovery, judicial and out-of-court reorganization, real estate and succession matters, for companies and individuals.

Customs Law

Defense of importers and exporters in customs audits and assessments, such as penalties replacing forfeiture of goods and fraudulent interposition, and guidance on tariff classification, customs valuation, detained goods and the Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) program.

Services

Specialized support for the tax challenges companies face

Tax settlements and installment plans

Assessment and handling of agreements to regularize tax debts, whether registered as overdue tax liabilities or under administrative or judicial dispute: settlements with the Attorney General of the National Treasury (PGFN) and the Federal Revenue Service, through public notices or individual proposals, state and municipal programs, and ordinary and special installment plans. The work weighs discounts, terms and guarantees against the prospects of the defense, so the client can decide with clarity, and follows the agreement through to full payment.

Tax reform readiness

Specialized support in preparing companies for Brazil’s new consumption taxes, IBS and CBS: assessing the impact on pricing, contracts, the supply chain and tax credits, reviewing operations and following the entire transition period.

Tax reform practice (in Portuguese) → Contact the firm →

Tax credit recovery and monetization

Identifying taxes paid unduly or in excess, recovering them through administrative and judicial channels, and defining the best way to use them: offset, refund, transfer or assignment of credits, where the law allows.
