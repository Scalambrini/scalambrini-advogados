The Firm

Advisory and litigation in four complementary areas.

Since its founding, Scalambrini Advogados has worked extensively in tax, corporate, customs and social security law, advising and representing companies of different sizes and sectors, as well as individuals.

The practice combines prevention and defense. In advisory work, transactions and risks are reviewed before decisions are made. In litigation, the firm handles administrative and judicial proceedings at every level, with strategy shaped by each matter’s impact on the client.

Service is direct and personalized, with close follow-up on every matter. Based on Avenida Engenheiro Luiz Carlos Berrini in São Paulo, the firm also works in Brasília.

Practice Areas

Practice areas

Tax Law

Advisory and litigation on federal, state and municipal taxes, including tax assessments, tax enforcement actions, annulment actions and writs of mandamus, at the administrative and judicial levels. Also covers ICMS tax incentives, tax settlements and individual income tax.

Corporate Law

Advice on corporate transactions, reorganizations and contracts, with attention to the tax effects of each decision, the liability of partners and officers, companies under judicial reorganization and the transition to Brazil’s new consumption taxes.

Customs Law

Defense of importers and exporters in customs audits and assessments, such as penalties replacing forfeiture of goods and fraudulent interposition, and guidance on tariff classification, customs valuation, detained goods and the Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) program.

Social Security Law

Payroll-based social security and third-party contributions, offsets and refunds, RAT and FAP classification, and the regularization of social security debts of companies and individuals.

Services

Specialized support for the tax challenges companies face

Tax reform readiness

Specialized support in preparing companies for Brazil’s new consumption taxes, IBS and CBS: assessing the impact on pricing, contracts, the supply chain and tax credits, reviewing operations and following the entire transition period.
