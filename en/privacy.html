Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Who handles the data

Scalambrini Sociedade Individual de Advocacia, CNPJ 60.294.581/0001-43, Av. Eng. Luiz Carlos Berrini, 1748, suite 1710, Cidade Monções, São Paulo, SP 04571-000, Brazil. Contact: contato@scalambrini.com.

What data we receive

The website requires no registration. The firm only receives the data you choose to send through the contact form, by email or by WhatsApp. Through the form: name, company (if provided), email, phone number (optional), area of interest and, if completed, a summary of the matter. The form asks you not to send documents or confidential information.

Along with your message, the form records information about where the contact came from: the page on which it was sent, the language, any campaign parameters in the page address (for example, utm_source and utm_campaign) and whether the email address is a business address or from a personal email provider. If you allow advertising measurement (see “Cookies and measurement”), the Google ad click identifier is also recorded, when available.

How we use it

To reply to your message, schedule meetings, assess the request and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal for services. Information about the origin of the contact helps the firm understand which channels and campaigns bring in enquiries. Processing is based on Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD), in particular on preliminary procedures related to a contract at the data subject’s request and on the firm’s legitimate interest (Article 7, items V and IX). The ad click identifier is recorded only with your consent (Article 7, item I).

Contact form and Formspree

Messages sent through the website’s form are received by Formspree, Inc., which acts as a data processor: the service receives the form and forwards it to the firm’s email. According to Formspree’s own documentation, its services are hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States, with encrypted databases and network traffic, and incoming messages go through automatic spam filtering. Sending the form therefore involves an international transfer of personal data under Article 33 of the LGPD.

On the plan used by the firm, Formspree keeps the message history for 30 days. If you prefer not to use the form, you can contact the firm directly by email or WhatsApp.

Professional confidentiality

Information about cases and legal matters received through the contact channels is kept under the professional confidentiality established by Brazil’s Statute of the Legal Profession (Law No. 8,906/1994).

Sharing and third-party services

The firm does not sell or share personal data for commercial purposes. The website relies on third-party services with their own policies: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts (fonts), Unsplash (images), Formspree (contact form) and, when you choose them, WhatsApp, email and Google Calendar booking. Google Analytics and Google Ads are used only with your permission, as described below.

Cookies and measurement

The website does not use its own cookies to identify visitors. The firm may use Google Analytics, to measure how the website is used, and Google Ads, to measure the results of advertising. These tools are loaded only after you give permission in the cookie notice, which separates the “analytics” and “advertising” categories; declining does not affect your browsing.

With advertising permission, the ad click identifier may accompany a message sent through the form, so that the firm can later tell Google Ads, without any other personal data, which enquiries led to an engagement. The firm does not use remarketing or personalized ads.

Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 12 months and can be changed at any time through the “Cookie preferences” link in the page footer, available whenever these tools are active.

Retention

Data is kept for as long as needed to respond to your message and to meet legal and professional obligations.

Your rights

You may at any time request confirmation of processing, access, correction or deletion of your data, among the other rights set out in Article 18 of the LGPD, including withdrawal of consent, by writing to contato@scalambrini.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the current version appears at the top of this page.
