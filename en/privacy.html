What data we receive

The website requires no registration. The firm only receives the data you choose to send by email, by WhatsApp or through the contact form, which opens your own email program: name, email, phone number and the content of your message.

How we use it

To reply to your message, schedule meetings and, where appropriate, prepare a fee proposal, in accordance with Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD).

Professional confidentiality

Information about cases and legal matters received through the contact channels is kept under the professional confidentiality established by Brazil’s Statute of the Legal Profession (Law No. 8,906/1994).

Sharing and third-party services

The firm does not sell or share personal data for commercial purposes. The website relies on third-party services with their own policies: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts (fonts) and, when you choose them, WhatsApp, email and Google Calendar booking.

Cookies

The website does not use its own cookies or any tracking or advertising tools.

Retention

Data is kept for as long as needed to respond to your message and to meet legal and professional obligations.

Your rights

You may at any time request confirmation of processing, access, correction or deletion of your data, among the other rights set out in Article 18 of the LGPD, by writing to contato@scalambrini.com.
